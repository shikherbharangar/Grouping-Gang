01 /

Destinations

Assign an open group to a number. The number stays yours, even when the group closes.

Create a tab group in Brave, then choose Assign group below. New groups can use free numbers when automatic assignment is enabled.

Existing and missing destinations keep their numbers. Automatic assignments can be undone for 30 seconds.

Moving to a collapsed group opens it. Moving between windows follows the destination.

Keep it local

No accounts. No analytics.
No browsing history saved.

Assignments and appearance live in this browser profile. Group and tab IDs are used only to keep destinations connected.

Reset & diagnostics

Reset clears assignments and appearance. Your browser tabs and groups remain open. A local copy of the previous registry is retained for troubleshooting.
